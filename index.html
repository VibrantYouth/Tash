How to play
  1. On your turn, play 1–4 cards face down. If no claim is open, tap a rank in the chooser panel to start a claim.
  2. While a claim is open, everyone must continue that same rank — you cannot switch to another rank until the claim is finished.
  3. A claim finishes when someone doubts it, or when everyone else passes.
  4. Doubt correct (the play was a bluff) → the whole pile goes to the bluffer's hand, and the doubter wins the round and throws the first card of the next claim.
  5. Doubt wrong (the play was true) → the whole pile goes to the doubter's hand, and the bluffer starts the next claim.
  6. If everyone passes, the pile is removed from the game and the turn goes to the next player, who starts the next claim.
  7. When a player empties their hand and their last claim is accepted, they finish — 1st, then everyone else keeps playing for 2nd and 3rd place, until only one player remains.
